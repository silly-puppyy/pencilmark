# Edges and corners

This page covers rules on a pair of adjacent cells (6.6) and on a 2×2 block (6.7).

## 6.6 `edge`

Exactly 2 orthogonally adjacent cells.

<div className="pm-kinds">

| `kind` | Fields | Meaning | Drawing |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kropki` | `color`: `"white"` \| `"black"` | White: \|a − b\| = 1. Black: one digit is double the other. A 1–2 pair may carry either color. | <Swatch name="edge-kropki-white" /> <Swatch name="edge-kropki-black" /> |
| `xv` | `letter`: `"X"` \| `"V"` | X: a + b = 10. V: a + b = 5. | <Swatch name="edge-xv-x" /> <Swatch name="edge-xv-v" /> |
| `difference` | `value` k | \|a − b\| = k. | <Swatch name="edge-difference" /> |
| `sum` | `sum` s | a + b = s. | <Swatch name="edge-sum" /> |
| `product` | `value` p | a · b = p. | <Swatch name="edge-product" /> |
| `ratio` | `value` k | one digit is k times the other. | <Swatch name="edge-ratio" /> |
| `greater` | – | `cells[0] > cells[1]`; the order matters. | <Swatch name="edge-greater" /> |

A numbered clue (`difference`, `sum`, `product`, `ratio`) must be made by some pair of different digits 1..D (the two
cells always share a row or column, so their digits differ); a value no pair makes is refused.

</div>

`kropki` white and `difference` 1 mean the same to a solver but draw differently; both exist on purpose (likewise
black / `ratio` 2 and X, V / `sum` 10, 5). Canonical writers that cannot tell them apart write `kropki` and `xv`.

## 6.7 `corner`

Exactly 4 cells forming a 2×2 block, written TL, TR, BL, BR.

<div className="pm-kinds">

| `kind` | Fields | Meaning | Drawing |
|---|---|---|---|
| `battenburg` | – | The 4 cells form a parity checkerboard: TL and BR share a parity, TR and BL the other. | <Swatch name="corner-battenburg" /> |
| `mathrax` | `op`: `"sum"` with `sum`; `"difference"`, `"product"` or `"ratio"` with `value` (the pair operators of 5.1, as on numbered edges); or `op`: `"even"` / `"odd"` | Each diagonal pair (TL–BR and TR–BL) combines to the clue (difference and ratio are larger over smaller). `even` / `odd`: all four digits have that parity. Drawn as the number and `+ − × ÷`, or E / O. | <Swatch name="corner-mathrax" /> |
| `exclude` | `value` | None of the 4 cells holds `value`. | <Swatch name="corner-exclude" /> |

</div>

Digits that must appear around a corner are a `quadruple` rule on the 4 cells (6.13); it is drawn as a
corner circle.
