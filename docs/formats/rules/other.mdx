# Other rules

This page covers the remaining rule families (6.13), vendor rules (6.15), what is not a rule (6.16), reserved names (6.17) and fog of war (6.19); custom rules (6.18) have [their own page](custom.mdx).

## 6.13 Other families

```json
{"type": "contains", "digits": [1, 9], "cells": ["r1c1", "r1c2", "r2c1", "r2c2"]}
{"type": "appears", "source": "r5c5", "cells": ["r5c6", "r5c7", "r5c8", "r5c9"]}
{"type": "clone", "regions": [["r1c1", "r1c2"], ["r9c8", "r9c9"]]}
{"type": "isomorphic", "regions": [["r1c1", "…81 cells…"], ["r1c11", "…81 cells…"]]}
{"type": "count", "each": 2, "cells": ["r1c1", "…"]}
{"type": "count", "kind": "circles", "cells": ["r1c1", "r4c7"]}
{"type": "distinct_count", "value": 2, "side": "top", "cells": ["r1c2", "r2c3", "r3c4"]}
{"type": "global_entropy"}
{"type": "equal_sum", "segments": [["r1c1", "r1c2"], ["r2c3"]]}
{"type": "vudoku", "cells": ["r2c2", "r1c2", "r2c3"]}
```

<div className="pm-kinds">

| `type` | Meaning | Drawing |
|---|---|---|
| `contains` | Every listed digit (a multiset) appears among `cells` at least as often as it is listed. On a 2×2 block it is the quadruple clue. | <Swatch name="contains-quadruple" /> |
| `appears` | The digit in `source` appears in at least one of `cells` (an in-cell arrow whose digit occurs again along its ray). |  |
| `clone` | ≥ 2 equal-sized ordered `regions`; corresponding cells hold equal digits. |  |
| `isomorphic` | 2 ordered `regions` (twins): one fixed relabelling of the digits maps every cell of the first region to the corresponding cell of the second. |  |
| `count` | `each` k: every digit appears exactly k times among `cells` (Double Sudoku rows). Or `counts` `[[digit, times], …]`: those digits appear exactly that often. Kind `circles`: a digit that appears among the cells appears exactly as many times as its own value. The cells of an `each` or `counts` rule MUST NOT also form a house (their digits repeat: a Double Sudoku row is a `count`, not a `row`); counting circles may sit on any cells. |  |
| `distinct_count` | Exactly `value` different digits among `cells`. With `side` it is an outside clue (ordered cells). |  |
| `global_entropy` | Every 2×2 window of existing cells holds a low, a middle and a high digit (thirds of 1..D; D divisible by 3). |  |
| `equal_sum` | Every segment (a cell set) has the same sum. For lines, use the `line` kinds `zipper`, `region_sum`, `double_arrow`. |  |
| `vudoku` | `cells` = [vertex, arm, arm], usually an L of three cells (the arms beside the vertex): the vertex digit is the sum or the difference of the two arm digits. | <Swatch name="vudoku" /> |

</div>

## 6.15 Vendor rules

`{"type": "com.example:my_rule", …}`: must-understand like every other rule; a reader that does not implement it
refuses the file.

## 6.16 What is not a rule

Variant names (Argyle, Windoku, …) are not rules: they are `meta.type` only. Colours, fonts and artwork are `style`
and `decorations` only.

## 6.17 Reserved

- `"type": "table"` and the operator `allowed` (explicit lists of allowed digit tuples): reserved and refused. Such
  lists make files large and cannot be drawn; [custom rules (6.18)](custom.mdx) replace them.
- `digits` as an object (`{"values": [0, …, 8], "symbols": {…}}`, with an `empty` sentinel) for digit sets other
  than 1..D, and digit-selection puzzles: reserved; refused in format_version 1.

## 6.19 Fog of war

```json
{"type": "fog", "lit": ["r5c5"]}
{"type": "fog", "lit": ["r1c1", "r1c2"], "triggers": [{"when": ["r1c1"], "light": ["r4c4", "r4c5"]}]}
```

The grid is covered in fog except the `lit` cells: everything visible at the start, givens included where the puzzle
shows them. A digit entered that matches the solution lights more:

- without `triggers`: the 3×3 block around it, clipped at the board edge;
- with `triggers`: only the triggers. Each lights its `light` cells once every cell of its `when` holds its digit (the
  setter's reveal pattern).

A file with fog MUST carry its `solution`: "a correct digit" is defined by it. Clues on fogged cells are hidden; a clue
on the edge of a lit cell, outside clues and rules stated for the whole board are seen. Fog changes no solution, but
it is a rule of play: the puzzle is solved from what the fog shows. A grader that cannot grade under fog refuses the
puzzle; it never grades the revealed grid. SudokuPad, f-puzzles and sudoku.coach store fog in their own ways (see their
documentation); pencilmark's readers translate them.
