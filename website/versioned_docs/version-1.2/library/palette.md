## Annotation colours

The named colours of step annotations and of the trace's colour layer ([Grade card](../formats/grade-card.md) sections 5 and 6), drawn over the cell at opacity 0.65 (light) / 0.5 (dark), over region shading and shaded cell shapes (odd / even, low / high: their fill shows through and their outline is drawn again above) and under lines, arrows, cages, dots, clues and digits. A cell with several colours is split into wedges from its centre, as SudokuPad draws them: n colours cut the cell's outline into n equal lengths, the first cut 25/360 of the way round clockwise from the top centre (two colours: a near-vertical diagonal).
